# Fox Points v2 — Proposal (PROPOSED, not locked, not public)

Written 2026-11-06 at the user's request. This is a proposal for the user to decide on — nothing here is promised to holders, posted anywhere, or locked in until the user says so.

## What is already locked (do not change without a deliberate decision)

- Points table: hold a fox 10, first-100 minters +20, X post with #NeonFoxAgents 5/day, invite a minter 10. Campaign tasks: follow 20, like 10, repost 15, comment 15, own post 5/day.
- Ranks: Pup 0–9, Fox 10–49, Alpha Fox 50+.
- Points are a score, not a token. They do NOT entitle anyone to tokens or payouts. (User's rule, 2026-11-01.)
- Roadmap: stages 1+2 only so far (stages-not-dates, weekly updates, points/roles/advisory votes). Later: capped BNB raffle from money actually received, holder-only companion mint (333), burn 3 → 1 Evolved Fox (max 158). Token deferred.
- Never promise: floor price, returns/APY, a dated token/airdrop, a game, or merch.

## The two catches (these decide how far any use case can go)

### 1. Points are farmable as built today
The campaign is click-to-complete on trust — a click awards points whether or not the X action happened. That is fine while points are just a score. The moment points are worth money, farming starts.

Rule for every money-linked use below: lean on the points that cannot be faked — holding a fox (on-chain), first-100 minter (on-chain), paid mints (on-chain). X-task points count only as a small bonus, or need real verification later (Zealy / Galxe / X API route) if farming becomes a problem.

### 2. The token cannot be funded yet
Max paid-mint revenue so far is about $117 (~0.15 BNB). A token launch needs roughly 18 BNB to graduate a bonding curve. So no dated airdrop and no amounts can be promised today. The airdrop direction can be stated ("if a token ever launches, points will count") — the amounts and the date cannot.

Changing the public line from "points do not entitle you to tokens" to "points will count toward a future token" is a deliberate decision. It must be announced clearly, not flipped quietly.

## Proposed uses, ranked

### 1. Future $NFOX token airdrop weight (PROPOSED — direction only)
- How: if a token ever launches, every holder gets a base share, and Fox Points multiply it. Alpha Fox gets the biggest multiplier, Fox next, Pup gets the base.
- Suggested multipliers (to decide, not locked): Pup 1x, Fox 1.5x, Alpha Fox 2x, with a cap so no single wallet eats the pool.
- Weighting: on-chain points count fully; X-task points count at a reduced weight (see Catch 1).
- Anti-sybil: every minting wallet holds a Binance Agent ID (1 per wallet, EOA-only), which is stronger protection than most airdrops have.
- Open: token funding, supply, snapshot date, exact multipliers, cap. Nothing dated, nothing with amounts, until funding is solved.

### 2. Companion mint priority, made concrete (PROPOSED — ready to lock, costs nothing)
- Companion collection is 333 supply, holder-only.
- Alpha Fox: guaranteed spot. Fox: early window. Pup: first-come-first-served after the windows.
- Needs no token and no money. Can be locked as soon as the companion is ready.

### 3. Evolved Fox forge queue (PROPOSED)
- When burn 3 → 1 Evolved Fox arrives (max 158 can ever exist), Alpha Fox holders forge first, then Fox, then Pup.
- The queue itself is the value — scarcity does the work, no extra promises needed.

### 4. Raffle weighting (PROPOSED — ready to lock, costs nothing)
- Points already buy raffle entries. Give Alpha Fox a multiplier on entries (suggested: Alpha 2x entries, Fox 1.5x, Pup 1x — to decide).
- Pot stays capped and funded only from paid revenue actually received.

### 5. Votes weighted by points (PROPOSED)
- Advisory votes today are one holder, one vote. Weighting by points means the people who actually show up decide more.
- First vote to try it on: naming the companion — Fox Pups / Neon Pups / Fox Cubs.

### 6. Cross-project loyalty (PROPOSED — the sleeper)
- Fox Points become the loyalty score across all thirteen mints. Alpha Fox gets early or free access to future launches — Rogbots and CORTEX paid phases, companion mints, whatever comes next.
- This is the one no competitor can copy, because they do not have thirteen launches. One score, thirteen doors.
- Open: exactly which launches, and whether the access is early window, guaranteed spot, or free. Needs a per-launch decision so it never promises supply that does not exist.

### 7. Spend points (PROPOSED — ready to lock, costs nothing)
- Let people burn points for extra raffle tickets.
- Gives points a use right now, and the sink stops early earners from sitting on a huge lead forever.
- Open: exchange rate (points per ticket) and whether spent points lower your rank.
